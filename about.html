The Physician & Founder

Meet the Doctor Behind Club Aayurveda

Dr. Sayali M. Deshmukh

BAMS | PGDCC | Ayurvedic Physician | Clinical Cosmetologist | Skin & Hair Specialist

Dr. Sayali M. Deshmukh is a qualified Ayurvedic physician and clinical cosmetologist with over five years of professional experience. She is the lead physician at Club Aayurveda and is committed to providing personalised Ayurvedic and herbal care.

Her key areas of expertise include skin health, hair care, gut health, digestive disorders and women’s wellness. She provides personalised care for common skin concerns such as acne, pigmentation, dryness, premature ageing and recurring skin problems. Her hair-care approach focuses on concerns including hair fall, dandruff, weak hair roots and poor scalp health.

Dr. Sayali also has a special interest in managing PCOD and PCOS through Ayurveda, dietary guidance and sustainable lifestyle modifications. She provides supportive Ayurvedic care for ovarian health and other hormone-related concerns following a detailed individual assessment. Where clinically appropriate, Panchakarma therapies may be incorporated into personalised treatment plans.

Her approach to gut problems and digestive disorders focuses on understanding digestion, dietary habits, lifestyle and the individual’s body constitution. She also has professional training in diet and obesity management and provides personalised plans for healthy weight management and metabolic wellness.

Rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach, she carefully evaluates each patient’s health concerns, Prakriti, lifestyle and wellness goals. Her treatment philosophy combines classical Ayurvedic principles, herbal care, clinical cosmetology, Panchakarma and practical lifestyle guidance. With a compassionate and patient-centred approach, Dr. Sayali aims to support long-term wellness through safe, responsible and personalised care.
