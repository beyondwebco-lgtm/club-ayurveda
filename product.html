"Shata" means 100, "Dhauta" means washed, and "Ghrita" refers to pure clarified butter. In classical Ayurvedic rasashastra, this revered therapeutic moisturizer is meticulously whipped and washed 100 consecutive times in copper vessels. Copper infuses trace restorative minerals while the repeated washing eliminates heavy oily odor, resulting in a cooling, velvety, luxurious cream that melts effortlessly into the dermis.
  • Skin Barrier Repair: Rich in essential fatty acids (Omega 3 & 9) to reinforce the lipid barrier.
  • Pitta Pacifying & Cooling: Instantly calms redness, skin irritation, sunburn, and rosacea flare-ups.
  • Deep Cellular Hydration: Micro-emulsified ghee molecules penetrate deep tissue without clogging pores.
  • Natural Glow: Promotes cellular renewal and smooths fine texture for a radiant Vedic glow.
1. Cleanse face with lukewarm water or a gentle botanical cleanser.
2. Mist face lightly with pure rose water or herbal hydrosol (Shata Dhauta Ghrita binds wonderfully with moisture).
3. Scoop a pea-sized amount using the clean golden applicator spoon.
4. Gently warm between fingertips and press onto face and neck in upward circular strokes until fully absorbed.
5. Recommended as a nightly restorative cream or as a targeted barrier treatment for dry zones.
Full Ingredients: Pure Clarified Cow Ghee (Ghrita), Purified Water (Jala), trace elemental copper micro-particles from traditional copper vessel washing.

100% Free from synthetic preservatives, parabens, sulfates, artificial fragrances, silicones, and petroleum derivatives.
Patch Test: As with any natural botanical formulation, apply a small dab behind the ear or inner wrist for 24 hours prior to initial use.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not allow water to enter the jar. Always use a dry spatula/spoon.
Warnings: For external cosmetic use only. Keep out of reach of children. Discontinue use if irritation develops.
